About UAS

The Uganda Astronomical Society is the national body for astronomy education, research, and outreach in Uganda.

History

The Uganda Astronomical Society was established to build a vibrant astronomy community in Uganda. From early star-gazing sessions at Makerere University to national outreach programmes reaching schools across the country, UAS has grown into the country's leading astronomical organisation.

UAS brings together amateur and professional astronomers, educators, students, and space enthusiasts under a shared vision: making astronomy accessible to every Ugandan.

Vision

A Uganda where every citizen has the opportunity to explore, understand, and appreciate the universe — and where Ugandan scientists contribute meaningfully to global astronomical knowledge.

Mission

To advance astronomical education, research, and public engagement in Uganda through community building, institutional partnerships, and accessible science communication.

Objectives

Education

Deliver astronomy education programmes in schools and universities across Uganda.

Research

Support and promote astronomical research by Ugandan scientists and students.

Outreach

Engage the public through observing events, talks, and media.

Community

Build a connected network of astronomers, educators, and enthusiasts.

Advocacy

Promote the value of astronomy and space science in national development.

Collaboration

Partner with regional and international astronomical organisations.

Governance

UAS is governed by a Board of Directors elected by the membership. The Board sets policy, approves programmes, and ensures the Society fulfils its mission. Day-to-day operations are managed through designated roles with specific capabilities — ensuring clear authority and accountability.

Board decisions are recorded as formal resolutions. When the Board resolves to create a role, assign capabilities, or approve a programme, those changes are applied transparently through the governance system.
