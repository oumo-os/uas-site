Our Programmes

Six permanent programme areas forming the backbone of UAS activity across Uganda.

Programme Architecture

Each permanent programme is led by a Programme Lead responsible for programme development, annual objectives, activity planning, member participation, technical coordination, reporting and continuity. Working groups are created when needed and dissolved once their purpose is complete — keeping UAS lean, flexible and activity-oriented.

Six Permanent Programme Areas

1. Astronomy Education

School and university programmes building astronomy literacy across Uganda.

2. Research & Citizen Science

Variable star observing, meteor counting, and collaboration with regional observatories.

3. Women in Astronomy

Mentorship, scholarships, and visibility for women pursuing physics and astronomy.

4. Public Outreach & Stargazing

Organised stargazing events, public lectures, and night-sky experiences for all Ugandans.

5. Space Science & Technology Awareness

Connecting astronomy to Uganda's broader space-science and technology ecosystem.

6. Indigenous Astronomy & Cultural Sky Knowledge

Preserving Ugandan and African astronomical knowledge and cultural sky traditions.
