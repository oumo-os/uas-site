Why Join UAS?

Membership gives you access to a community and resources dedicated to advancing astronomy in Uganda.

Observing Sessions

Join monthly public observing nights and special celestial event viewings across Uganda.

Knowledge Base

Access articles, observing reports, educational materials, and member-only content.

Community Network

Connect with astronomers, educators, students, and enthusiasts across the country.

Workshops & Training

Participate in telescope handling, astrophotography, data analysis, and research methods.

Research Opportunities

Collaborate on projects, contribute to publications, and present at UAS events.

Global Connections

Access partnerships with international astronomical organizations and events.

What to Expect

From sign-up to your first observing session — here's the journey

1

Create Your Account

Sign up with your name, email, and choose a membership category (Regular, Student, or Honorary). No fees required.

2

Complete Your Profile

Add your institution, location, bio, and interests. This helps other members find you and suggests relevant events.

3

Explore Events & Programmes

Browse upcoming observing sessions, workshops, and lectures. RSVP to events that interest you.

4

Join the Community

Connect with fellow members, join working groups, and participate in discussions through the resolution forum.

5

Contribute & Grow

Share your knowledge, contribute articles, assist with outreach, and help advance astronomy in Uganda.
