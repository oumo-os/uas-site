National Policy Alignment

UAS operates within Uganda's emerging national space-science framework, complementing government institutions and aligning with national development priorities.

STI Secretariat

Uganda's Science, Technology and Innovation Secretariat under the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation coordinates national science policy, including space science.

Aeronautics & Space Science Bureau

The dedicated bureau for aeronautics and space science within the STI framework — UAS engages as a public-science partner supporting national objectives.

National Development Plan IV

NDP IV identifies science, technology and innovation as drivers of Uganda's socio-economic transformation — astronomy contributes to STEM capacity and public scientific literacy.

USD 500 Billion Vision

Uganda's aspiration for a USD 500 billion economy by 2040 requires investment in human capital and STEM — UAS contributes by building scientific curiosity and technical capacity.
