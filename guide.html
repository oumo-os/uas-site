How to use the system

Everything a logged-in member needs: finding things, taking part, sharing content, leading teams and reviewing work. Sections outside your role stay hidden.

You're browsing as a guest

This guide is written for logged-in members — log in (or join the Society) to see the parts that apply to your roles.

1. Your dashboard

  • Overview — calendar (published events feed in automatically), recent pending items, your assignments, your submissions and activity.
  • Pending — everything awaiting review. Needs my action shows only items you can personally approve or vote on.
  • Programmes — the teams you belong to or lead (see §5).
  • Create — propose articles, events, programmes and projects (see §4).
  • Votes & Decisions, Finance, Members, Groups & Meetings, Mail — appear according to your roles and capabilities.

2. Finding things

  • Programmes — the Society's standing workstreams, each with its lead, projects, events and team.
  • Events — upcoming events plus a month calendar view; past events stay browsable. Open an event to RSVP, join the waitlist or add it to your personal calendar.
  • Knowledge — published articles, observing reports and educational pieces.
  • Members — who holds which office; vacant seats show an Express interest button.

3. Taking part

  • RSVP to an event from its page (members) — or sign up as a guest with just a name and email, no account needed.
  • Full event? Join the waitlist — you're promoted automatically when a place frees up.
  • Want to work on something? Open a programme, look at its projects, and contact the project team — or apply to join the programme and a lead will place you.
  • After attending, organizers mark you as attended — that's how participation history is built.

4. Sharing content: the approval pipeline

Everything you compose goes through review before it appears publicly. Track yours under Overview → My Submissions; reviewers work from Pending. You can edit your own drafts anytime from the item's details view.

Article

You write→Submitted→Reviewer approves→Publisher publishes→Live in Knowledge

Event

You propose→Submitted→Approved→Published→Open for RSVPs + on calendars

Project

You propose (optionally inside a programme)→Submitted→Approved→Published→Public project page + team

Programme

You propose→Draft (only you see it)→Activated→Public programme
  • Rejected or sent back? You'll get the reason by notification. Edit the item, then Revise & Resubmit from its details view.
  • Editing a published item goes live immediately — double-check dates and venues.

5. Leading a programmeProgramme leads

Teams live in projects, not in programmes. Your job: run the projects and events inside your programme.

  • Dashboard → Programmes → Manage on your programme card.
  • Projects & Teams — expand a project's Team to add participants, change anyone's role (Member, Coordinator, Lead…), or remove them. Start new work with New Project — it's pre-filed under your programme.
  • Events & Attendance — expand an event's Attendance to see RSVPs and guests, mark attendance after the event, and remove guest signups. Your events also appear here for editing.
  • Applications — approve or reject people asking to join; approvals notify the applicant automatically.
  • You can edit any event or project in your programme, and the programme's own details (Edit button on the card).

6. Reviewing workReviewers

  • Work in Pending → Needs my action. Open an item for full details and a public preview.
  • Approve moves it one step forward; Publish/Activate makes it public; Reject always asks for a reason — it goes to the author, so be specific and kind.
  • Rejected items return to the author as drafts; approving a draft pulls it into review as-is.
  • Authors are notified at every step — you don't need to chase anyone.

7. GovernanceVoters & officers

  • Votes & Decisions — resolutions (formal votes), polls (quick consultations) and delegations (proxy voting when you're away).
  • Meeting attendance and minutes live under Groups & Meetings.
  • Vacant offices are listed on the Members page — express interest to be considered.

8. Dues & financeMembers

  • Your member class (Regular, Student, Honorary…) sets your annual dues — see Members → Dues for the schedule.
  • Keep your membership in good standing: some actions require it.
  • Officers with finance access record income/expenses under Finance; every record keeps an audit trail.

9. Inbox, notifications & mail

  • The bell icon and dashboard Inbox carry approvals, decisions and mentions — click through to act.
  • Office mail (info@, programmes@, …) is available if your role grants an inbox; compose and reply from the Mail tab.
  • Event discussions happen in each event's chatroom, not by email.

10. Troubleshooting

  • Page looks outdated? Hard-refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac). The site caches aggressively for speed.
  • Button missing? Actions appear only when your roles allow them — check Your access above, or ask a lead/approver.
  • Save failed? The message explains why (usually a permission or a missing required field). Drafts are never lost — just retry.
  • Locked out or wrong role? Contact the Secretary via the contact page or membership@astronomy.ug.
